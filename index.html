WHAT WE BELIEVE

A game should leave room
for players to find their own way.

Choice

A meaningful choice always leaves something behind. Knowing when to press on and when to return is both a mechanic and a measure of character.

Growth

Numbers may rise, but understanding should deepen too. Learn more about the world, and the next decision ought to change.

Rebirth

Beginning again is not returning to zero. What one life gained—and what it regretted—should leave a trace in the next.
