OFFICIAL POLICY

Privacy Policy

This page describes how Infans Studio handles information for its games and websites.

Effective Date

August 19, 2026

Who We Are

Brand: Infans Studio

Legal name: Shanghai Infans Digital Technology Co., Ltd.

Scope and Account Status

Infans may offer one account for multiple games. The account and cloud features described below apply only to products that visibly state that those features are enabled. Until an account-deletion feature has passed its operational checks and is shown as available in a product, we do not represent it as active; you may contact us instead.

Information We Process

  • When you use Google sign-in: Google's stable account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image. We do not receive or process your Google password.
  • Game data: browser-local saves and settings and, for enabled Google accounts, cloud saves, completed-run records, and replays.
  • Security and operations data: opaque session identifiers, expiry and activity timestamps, game identifier, and limited device or platform information needed to protect and operate the service.
  • Support messages and attachments you choose to send us.

Accounts, Guests, and Games

A single Infans account may be used across several games. Each game's records are logically separated, but games served from the same web origin remain within one technical trust domain; this is not the same as isolation between unrelated websites.

Guest storage differs by game. In No Interest, guest progress stays only in that browser on that device and is not uploaded to our server. If another game visibly offers server-side guest saves, abandoned server guest data may be removed after 180 days without activity.

How We Use Information

  • to sign you in and provide saves, synchronization, recovery, completed-run history, and session controls where enabled;
  • to maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and improve stability and user experience;
  • to respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not currently publish player rankings or account-linked game records.

Storage, Security, and Service Providers

Browser-local game data may be stored in IndexedDB. Signed-in sessions may use Secure, HttpOnly cookies so browser scripts cannot read the session credential. Google Identity Services handles Google authentication under Google's own terms. Enabled cloud features communicate with our self-hosted service at api.ifansstudio.com, using infrastructure stored on our NAS. We use reasonable access controls, transport security, and operational safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and Deletion

Live account and game records are kept while needed to provide enabled features, until you delete the account, or as otherwise required by law. When verified account deletion is available and successfully completed, current server data is deleted immediately. Residual copies may remain in encrypted server backups or snapshots for no longer than 30 days before rotation and are not returned to normal service. Server-side guest records, where a game offers them, may be purged after 180 days without activity. We may retain minimal records where required for security, dispute resolution, or law.

Your Choices and Rights

Where shown as available in an account-enabled product, you can export your account identity and per-game current saves, backups, history, completed-run records, and replays; revoke other signed-in sessions; and request account deletion. Deletion requires a fresh Google verification matching the signed-in account and an explicit confirmation phrase. The product deletion flow also clears the local browser copy it manages; you can additionally clear site data through your browser. Subject to applicable law, you may also ask us to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, requests, or account-data help, please contact: capoo@ifansstudio.com
